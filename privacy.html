Privacy Policy

EBS Rclone Backup

EBS Rclone Backup is a private application used for internal backup and disaster recovery purposes.

Google Drive Data

The application uses Google Drive authorization to upload, access, verify, manage, and delete backup files belonging to the authorized Google account.

Use of Information

Google user data accessed by this application is used solely for performing backup and recovery operations.

The application does not sell, rent, distribute, or share Google user data with third parties.

Data Storage

Backup files are stored in the Google Drive account explicitly authorized by the application owner.

Access and Revocation

Access to Google Drive may be revoked at any time through the security settings of the authorized Google Account.

Application Use

This application is intended for private internal use and is not offered as a public service.
